Boeing 747‑200 “The Kimbap Queen”
Role: Legacy jumbo jet
Seats: 366–452
Range: 12,700 km

“The Kimbap Queen” is GlazedKimpabAir’s iconic restored 747‑200, used for special heritage flights and aviation showcases. GlazedKimpabAir started with ambition and a used Boeing 747‑200 bought from a Middle Eastern cargo carrier for the price of a Seoul apartment. The founders wanted one thing: “A plane big enough to make people notice us.” The 747-200 became the airline’s first aircraft, first long‑haul machine, first everything. It flew Seoul → Bangkok → Dubai → London with fuel stops, delays, and drama, but it built the airline’s identity. Spotters called it: “The Kimbap Queen.”
