Airbus A350‑1000
Role: Flagship long‑haul aircraft
Seats: 350–410
Range: 16,100 km
Aircraft type: Wide‑body

The Airbus A350‑1000 is GlazedKimpabAir’s premium long‑haul flagship, offering ultra‑quiet cabins, advanced fuel efficiency, and next‑generation comfort.

GlazedKimpabAir evaluated several long‑haul options in 2023 and chose the A350‑1000 after a private demonstration in Toulouse.

Two aircraft were ordered in early 2024. The first was delivered in October 2024 and named “The Sky Sword.” It launched on the Seoul–Copenhagen route as the airline’s new prestige service.
