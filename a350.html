Airbus A350‑1000
Role: Flagship long‑haul aircraft
Seats: 350–410
Range: 16,100 km

Aircraft type: Wide-Body
The Airbus A350‑1000 is GlazedKimpabAir’s premium long‑haul flagship, offering ultra‑quiet cabins, advanced fuel efficiency, and next‑generation comfort. GlazedKimpabAir needed a real long‑haul flagship, tested options in 2023, and chose the A350‑1000 after a private demo in Toulouse. They ordered two in early 2024, got the first one in October, named it “The Sky Sword,” and launched it on the Seoul–Copenhagen route.
