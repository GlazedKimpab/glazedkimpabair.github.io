Lockheed L‑1011‑1 TriStar
Role: Wide‑body tri‑jet
Seats: 250–330
Range: 6,100 km

The Lockheed L‑1011‑1 TriStar is one of the most advanced and beloved tri‑jets ever built — famous for its smooth ride, quiet cabin, and futuristic engineering.

How GlazedKimpabAir acquired it:

The TriStar was never meant to join the fleet. It started as a rumor — a retired L‑1011 sitting in storage in the Philippines, untouched for years, but still in surprisingly good condition. When GlazedKimpabAir heard about it, the founders sent a small team to inspect it. The aircraft was dusty, sun‑faded, and missing a few panels… but the moment they stepped inside, they knew.

“This isn’t a plane. This is history.”

The deal was sealed within 48 hours. The TriStar was ferried to Seoul after a massive systems check, flying one of the last L‑1011 revenue‑style flights in the world. Aviation fans tracked it the entire way.

Why we wanted it:
• Legendary S‑duct engine
• One of the smoothest rides ever built
• Rare, iconic, and loved by spotters
• Fits GKA’s retro‑modern fleet identity


Today, the TriStar is undergoing a careful restoration and repaint into GlazedKimpabAir colors. It will soon operate special heritage flights, aviation tours, and retro‑themed events.

Spotter nickname:
“The Star Queen.”
