About us

Starting out

The company spent two years developing its brand, fleet, strategy and operational network before starting operations in 2026. At launch, the airline introduced a fleet of the Airbus A320-200, A330-900neo and A320neo, focusing on medium- and long-haul routes. Although a relatively new carrier, GlazedKimpabAir had built a well-organised, operational fleet by June 2026.

Maintaining the fleet

Modern vs. nostalgic and aged aircraft

Main article: “Operation: Restoration”

The airline operates efficient, modern aircraft. However, GlazedKimpabAir's CEO said in an interview that he wanted to make room for older aircraft such as the Airbus A300-600, A340-500 and -600, A380-800, Boeing 747-200, Boeing 767-300ER and the McDonnell Douglas MD-11. They would all be bought or restored, then go through serious maintenance and testing to make sure they are fully operational again. This plan was called “Operation: Restoration”.

Joining and founding GlobalWorld Alliance

Main article: GlobalWorld Alliance

On May 5, 2026, the airline teamed up with Lerak's Airways to form a new alliance called GeoWorld Alliance. It was later renamed GlobalWorld Alliance. Within two months, the alliance had more than five airlines in the group.

Potential competitors

GlazedKimpabAir is expanding quickly, and a few airlines could compete with it:

  1. Korean Air: South Korea's flag carrier, now even stronger after acquiring Asiana Airlines.
  2. Aeguk Air: another strong South Korean airline with a big network.
  3. Jeju Air: the biggest low-cost carrier in South Korea.
  4. Scandinavian Airlines: also Danish, so it could compete for that reason.

Fleet acquisition from Asiana Airlines

Main post: Asiana Airlines agreement

In June 2026, GlazedKimpabAir took an interest in the aircraft of Asiana Airlines, whose future was due to be decided at the end of the year because of its merger with Korean Air. On August 1, 2026, GlazedKimpabAir decided to take over most of Asiana's fleet.

The deal covered 69 of the 74 aircraft in Asiana's entire fleet. Most of them were used, with an average age of 10.9 years. Many of the aircraft GlazedKimpabAir took were already on order in its own fleet plans. It also took over 27 pending orders that Asiana could not receive in time, and moved them to 15 more Airbus A350-900 orders and 12 more Airbus A321neo orders.

The A321neo and A350-900 were already on order, but the airline also took all 8 operating Boeing 777-200ER from Asiana, which had never been part of its fleet plans. Superjumbos like the Airbus A380-800 were also acquired, bringing the airline's planned total to 15 A380s. Most aircraft are expected to be delivered between late 2026 and 2028, though the A380 may be on hold.

On August 1, 2026, Hanjin Group and GlazedKimpab Group agreed to transfer a large number of Asiana aircraft, including pending deliveries that were never delivered in time. The whole deal was worth 25.8 billion won (18,116,760 USD).
