McDonnell Douglas MD‑11
Role: Long‑haul tri‑jet
Seats: 285–323
Range: 12,455 km

The MD‑11 is GlazedKimpabAir’s newest addition — a rare, powerful tri‑jet that blends retro aviation charm with long‑haul capability. The aircraft came from a cargo airline in California that had just retired its last MD‑11. The moment the aircraft was listed, GlazedKimpabAir’s founders jumped on a flight to negotiate in person.

The seller said:
“If you can get it out of here by the weekend, it’s yours.”

So the team worked nonstop — paperwork, inspections, repainting, and a full systems check — all in under 72 hours.

On the third day, the MD‑11 lifted off for Seoul wearing a temporary white coat and a small GlazedKimpabAir sticker on the nose. Spotters went crazy.

Why we bought it:
• It’s iconic
• It’s powerful
• It’s a tri‑jet — and tri‑jets are aviation culture
• It fits GKA’s “retro‑modern” identity perfectly

Once it arrived in Seoul, it immediately entered a full repaint and cabin refresh. The MD‑11 will soon operate special long‑haul showcase flights and aviation‑themed events.

Spotter nickname:
“The Triple Queen.”
