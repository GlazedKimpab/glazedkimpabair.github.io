Airbus A330‑900neo
Role: Efficient long‑haul aircraft
Seats: 260–300
Range: 13,300 km

The A330‑900neo is a fuel‑efficient widebody ideal for medium and long‑haul routes, offering modern cabins and excellent operating economics. GlazedKimpabAir wanted to compete with Korean Air and Asiana on long‑haul premium routes.

We needed:
– Range
– Comfort
– Fuel efficiency
– A modern flagship

The A330‑900neo became our first true “luxury” aircraft.

Why it joined:
– Perfect for Europe and Oceania
– Lower cost than the 787
– Airbus offered a sweetheart financing deal
– Beautiful cabin + mood lighting

Passenger nickname:
“The Cloud Lounge.”
