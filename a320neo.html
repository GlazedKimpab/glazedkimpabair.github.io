GlazedKimpabAir Home Fleet
Airbus A320neo
Role: Short‑haul and regional narrowbody
Seats: 150–180
Range: 6,300 km

The Airbus A320neo is GlazedKimpabAir’s efficient workhorse for domestic and regional routes, offering excellent fuel savings and a quiet, modern cabin. GlazedKimpabAir wanted to dominate domestic Korea and short‑haul Asia. We needed something modern, quiet, and efficient. The A320neo was the obvious choice. Why it joined: - Perfect for Seoul → Busan, Jeju, Fukuoka, Shanghai - Low noise footprint (good for Gimpo) - Cheap to operate - Easy to maintain It quickly became our most common aircraft. Spotter nickname: “The Neon Noodle.”
