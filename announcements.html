  • Aug 1, 2026

    Asiana fleet agreement signed

    ello, this is the CEO of GlazedKimpabAir. On the 1st of August, 2026, Hanjin Group & GlazedKimpab Group have decided and agreed on transferring a big amount of Asiana aircraft including taking their pending deliveries that were never delivered in time.

    All this for a 25.8 billion won deal (18,116,760 USD). The agreement will consist of used/new Airbus A321neo, Airbus A330-300, Airbus A350-900, Airbus A380-800, and Boeing 777-200ER.

    We have agreed to by 69 of their 94 aircraft (including the ones on order), which is roughly 73.4% acquired from Asiana Airlines' entire fleet. Asiana Cargo's remaining fleet of parked a Boeing 747-400BDSF & a Boeing 747-400F (SCD) will happily be delivered to GlazedKimpab Cargo next year.

    The aircraft are currently pending maintenance (as of August 2026), and are expected to be delivered late 2026 to 2028.

    We will note however, that the Airbus A380-800 will have to wait, due to the A380 retirement.

    We will be taking pending deliveries for the Airbus A321neo & the Airbus A350-900, that may be delivered in late 2028.

    Thank you Asiana for this wonderful agreement! Read the full story.
